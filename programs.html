Program 01 · The roof decides everything

Roof & shading assessment

A solar array lasts decades and a roof does not. Putting panels on a roof with five years left means removing and reinstalling them later at real cost, so we check the roof first and will tell you to replace it before we quote. Shading is measured on site, not estimated from satellite imagery.

  • Homeowners
  • No obligation
  • Roof access

Program 02 · Sized to your bills

System design & estimates

Systems get oversized because larger systems pay better commission. We size to twelve months of your actual bills, show the production model with its assumptions visible, and are explicit that it is a model — weather varies, usage changes, and net metering rules are set by policy rather than by us.

  • Per design
  • Homeowners
  • Quoted in writing
  • 12 months of bills

Program 03 · Licensed and inspected

Permitted installation

Florida has specific wind load requirements and local authorities inspect for them. Work is done by licensed contractors, permits are pulled, inspections are attended, and penetrations are flashed properly — a leak two years later is the part nobody budgets for and the part that ruins the project.

  • 1 to 3 days
  • Homeowners
  • Included in quote
  • Permit approval

Program 04 · Not live until approved

Interconnection & monitoring

A system cannot legally be energised until the utility approves interconnection, and that timeline is theirs rather than ours. Afterwards, monitoring matters: a failed optimiser or a shaded string can cost a quarter of production for months before anyone notices from a bill alone.

  • Ongoing
  • Installed systems
  • Included initially
